May 15, 1941, Thursday

Skrzypek and Fabisiak went to Oran for the court hearing of
Lt. Szlawski and Cpl. Soja; both were acquitted. Kula again
got into a brawl with Wawrzyszek.

May 16, 1941, Friday

Cpl. Soja arrived at the camp and has been assigned to our
hall. Rumors are going around that France has ceded bases in Syria, but
some claim it was to the Germans and others to the English; for certain we know nothing
because the radio is broken again.

May 17, 1941, Saturday

The French newspapers report that the English bombed
air bases in Syria because German aircraft were stationed there.
We receive 25 francs each from the Red Cross. The French conduct a medical
inspection.

May 18, 1941, Sunday

We had the service at 10 o'clock because the children had their
First Holy Communion, and that is why it was changed for us, but there were more
people in the church. In the afternoon an unfinished match was held with Saida
because a legionnaire got into a fight with a player from Saida over a foul;
the game was very good despite our loss.
