September 17, 1940, Tuesday
We are waiting for demobilization, without result.

September 18, 1940, Wednesday
In the morning Fatyga hands out the ID papers of those newly promoted. They were
promoted to the rank of corporal-platoon [plut.]: Fatyga Jan, Rej Józef and Sadowski Stanisław,
and one more veteran corporal from Żurawica. To the rank of corporal a great many
senior privates were appointed, some who had been through the NCO school and some without it, depending on
what each man reported, because everything here is done on trust. Among them was
Kacperek Szczepan, Kowalski, Liberadzki and many others. With Seidel
some mistake occurred, because he was issued a new ID but with the same
rank. The appointment order is dated June 15, 1940.
I sign for demobilization, receiving pay for 1 month. After deductions
for food and the money that came from Argeles, I was left with 220
francs.
