December 15, 1939. Warrant Officer Czapiński caught the first louse. I enrolled in the drama circle and was given the role of Kacper, one of the Three Kings. Walczak got hold of a bottle of delicious wine from somewhere.

December 16. A transport arrives from Belgium (about 150 men); we welcome them with a band. I receive an invitation to spend the holidays in Lille from a certain Mr. Stanisław Budynek, whom I happened to meet in a café and, while drunk, gave him my address. I don't know what to do.

At the drama circle meeting my role was changed to Balthazar.

I wrote back to Mr. Budynek that I would not be coming for the holidays. We arranged ourselves a magnificent bath in little tubs used for carrying coal. Second Lieutenant Łowczowski lent me 50 francs.

December 17, Sunday

I got up in good spirits, singing. I was at High Mass in church. The band played and the priest gave an uplifting sermon. After the service the band gave a concert on the square. In the afternoon Gołębiowski and Szpak visited me, not counting the regular guests, Corporal Pawłowski and Corporal Matyjasik. There was also another corporal whose surname I don't know, a sailor, a solid fellow. We had food and drink beyond counting. The frost was quite severe and our coal ration was greatly reduced.
