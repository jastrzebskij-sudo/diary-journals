We waited for departure until evening, and nothing came of it.

January 12, 1941, Sunday
During Holy Mass in Ste Marthe the Polish priest gave the sermon (he
is not a good preacher). On the way back we met a French lady of our
acquaintance who was going out shopping. We walked her home. And
we stayed at their place hunting birds, without success. Yesterday many
Poles arrived at the camp near Nîmes, among them Sgt.
Gbur.
On January 9, Józek Rej, Szatan, and Kuleszka left for Gardanne to
work in the mine. Once again, our departure did not take place.

January 13, 1941, Monday
We (those who are leaving) were supposed to receive overcoats, but
somehow this changed, and only Zarychta received one — of course
with the help of a lie, as everything nowadays seems to happen. To our
recreation room hostesses have been assigned. There are quite a few
games. A professor gives French language lessons.

January 17, 1941, Saturday
We receive orders to depart. I went to say goodbye to the French and to
acquaintances. They strongly advised me against this trip. That evening
