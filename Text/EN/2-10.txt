January 13, 1940, Saturday

Corporal Kanigowski arrives by car from Paris; I ran into him by chance in front of camp headquarters. We went to see Warrant Officer Sepkowski and agreed that we would go to Campaniac. He spent the night at my place, on the table, on which we laid two straw mattresses. In the evening the "castle men" gathered.

January 14, 1940, Sunday

We drive to Campaniac with Kanigowski, Warrant Officer Sepkowski, one sergeant, and one civilian. We stopped at Staff Sergeant Kurnik's, who is the chief of the quartermaster company. Whole companies came for the service, so did everyone from the castle column. Kanigowski went to fetch Sagański. And just before departure, Świtajewski showed up.

We were at a café for cognac, then continued it at the billet. We return to Coetquidan by 12 o'clock. I am completely drunk.

The Coetquidan orchestra gave a concert in front of headquarters. Right after the concert I say goodbye to Kanigowski, who leaves for Paris, while I go off to perform the nativity play. We are missing Tychowski, Iwański, and Raczkowski.
