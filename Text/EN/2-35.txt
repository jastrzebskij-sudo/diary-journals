May 9, 1940, Thursday
I spend the whole day in Ira's company and it seems to me that I am in love. In the evening I go for another walk.

May 10, 1940, Friday
The Germans attacked Holland, Belgium and Luxembourg.
Before dawn an air-raid alarm woke us. After some time German airplanes flew over, which were greeted by English anti-aircraft artillery and fighter planes. Over the Lemay colony one English fighter fought a battle against 3 German bombers. One German bomber was shot down. Air-raid alarms continued all day, but I am more occupied with love.

May 11, 1940, Saturday
Alarms all day, as on the previous day, and the whole day similar to yesterday's.

May 12, 1940, Sunday
After the service, which I attended with Ira, I went with Mr. Ciemniak around the cafés, and so we visited about 5 Polish cafés. The Englishmen left for Belgium yesterday. In the evening we go for a walk with Ira and her friend. We spent this evening very pleasantly and properly.
