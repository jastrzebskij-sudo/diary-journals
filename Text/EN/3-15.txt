After Mass the company returned to the barracks singing. In the afternoon a football match took place between our team and the Saïda team. The match was very interesting, and our side played well despite losing 4–3 in favor of Saïda. In the evening Cierkosz, being tipsy, insulted Fatyga by saying that he was no platoon sergeant.

March 17, 1941, Monday
A report was made, since Fatyga reported Cierkosz for yesterday's incident. Cierkosz was punished with 5 days' detention for improper conduct.

March 18, 1941, Tuesday
Vice-Consul Rzepecki arrived from Oran; apparently he was supposed to give some money to certain people, but I don't know if anyone actually received any.

March 19, 1941, Wednesday
The company numbers over 190 men, but today only about 20 remained for activities, so the lieutenant dismissed even that remainder. I don't even know where these people are hiding out, since they are nowhere to be seen.
