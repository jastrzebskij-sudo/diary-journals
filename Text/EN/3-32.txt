May 4, 1941, Sunday

Like all previous ones, with a match played between
our team and the star (Arabs) team, 2-1 in our favor. Wicek
Zarychta and Dziadkowicz treat us to supper and we are in good spirits.

May 5, 1941, Monday

The radio was repaired, which brought us comforting news. All
evening I play "kupki" [a dice/card game] and won quite a lot. The company commander
awarded certain items of clothing to some of the most needy, and
again not everyone is satisfied. With Zarychta and Dziadkowiec we again
feast.

May 6, 1941, Tuesday

Kosiński received some position in the officers' casino.

May 7, 1941, Wednesday

For several days it has been cold and raining, and everyone asks
themselves where those African heatwaves are, since in Poland May was warmer.
The company has switched to civilian food supply, so now they buy for us
using ration cards. Many of my comrades have had certain items cut from their rations; I had
oil cut from mine, and because of this I had a bit of unpleasantness. I gave the oil
to Kosiński, and what he did with it I do not know, but that was still before
