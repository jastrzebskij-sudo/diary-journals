On the way this gentleman introduced himself as Zygmunt Krosnowski. I showed him my identification from the Castle Company and the driving license I had received in the 3rd Armored Battalion.

The car was a CHEVROLET limousine. I felt a bit awkward driving it at first, but after a while I got the hang of it. Mr. Krosnowski introduced me to his brother-in-law, Deputy Minister Tyszka, who within an hour arranged a photograph and passport for me. People sometimes waited days for a passport.

From Czerniowce we set out with two cars; in the first rode Mr. Tyszka and Mr. Krosnowski, with an engineer driving his own car whose surname I don't remember, and in the second I drove Mrs. Tyszkowa with her son. We reached Bucharest safely, where we stopped at the Hotel Nissa on Boulevard Elissabeta. The first night I slept in the hotel, and for the rest of my stay I slept in the car, which at night I would drive out and park
