March 11, 1941, Tuesday
Lt. Piątkowski returned and briefed us on the situation; the financial situation in particular looks very bad. For now we are to receive 50 centimes a day.

March 12, 1941, Wednesday
Some French officer arrived and is drawing up personnel lists; we don't know what for — perhaps our authorities know but are keeping quiet. Presumably this is French counterintelligence looking for German saboteurs among us.

March 13, 1941, Thursday
A day without any events.

March 14, Friday
A new group of Poles arrived, about 30 men, among them Sergeant Gbur and Czarnota, Corporals Wołk J., Słowiarski (Zagłoba), and a few others from Ste Marthe. There are new assignments, and once again my platoon commander is Sergeant Gbur.

March 15, 1941, Saturday
In the morning, a hunt for bedbugs, of which there are plenty. After dinner, choir rehearsal in church, which went well.
