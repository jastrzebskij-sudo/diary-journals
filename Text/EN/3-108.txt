The situation with water is very bad, because what they bring us is too little for washing clothes and ourselves, and on top of that the lack of soap is badly felt, as is the lack of any container for washing. Many in the company already have lice. Today at noon I stole a little water and washed my laundry, not with soap but with a washing powder called here "Lessive" — it removes dirt very well, but it also loosens the threads, and a shirt won't survive more than 3 washes, and we only have one shirt each. So everyone limits himself to washing once a month. So how, under these conditions, could one avoid getting lice.
