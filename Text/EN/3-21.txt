April 8, 1941, Monday, and April 9, 1941, Tuesday

Very scant news from the Balkan front seems to confirm the German advance. The Yugoslavs were said to have entered Albania.

April 10, 1941, Wednesday

The radio was sent back to Oran to be exchanged for a better one. French newspapers report the capture by the English of Massawa, an Italian port in Eritrea.

In the barracks we are doing holiday cleaning, whitewashing the rooms, burning out bedbugs, and so on.

April 11, 1941, Thursday

It is very beautiful in the ravine by the little river; whenever I have a free moment I spend time there. There are countless flowers there, from which we make bouquets to decorate the rooms. We receive relief funds from the Red Cross totaling 37 francs, and besides that I got 10 francs for doing the record-keeping a few days ago.

April 12, 1941, Good Friday

It did not pass in accordance with Catholic principles but rather with drinking and brawling. Jasiński, drunk, struck Aspirant Krowiński in the face, probably because Krowiński was simply the aspirant most hated by everyone.
