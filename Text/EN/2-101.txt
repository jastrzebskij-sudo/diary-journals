A demobilization took place in Ste Marthe, and apparently several of
our men were demobilized, while the chief sat in on the demobilization
and collected a certain percentage from these gentlemen for the
company's holiday fund. In this way 10,800 francs were collected. This
demobilization is clearly a sham — it's as if it were an unofficial way of
handing out relief payments; it's a pity that it's always the same people
doing it, over and over.
From this demobilization money that the chief collected, we received
50 francs each.

On Saturday, December 21, Jasio and I were at the cinema with our
acquaintances for the second time.

December 23, 1940, Monday
At the Polish Red Cross I received a pair of boots and a scarf. And this
happened as if five minutes to twelve, because the very next day, or
rather that night, the first snow fell and covered the ground with a
blanket 20 cm thick — and I would have looked fine indeed in my
windbreaker.
