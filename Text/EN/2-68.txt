August 16, Friday

I was with Wolski and Rej in the small town, shopping. You can get everything here and the people are very well disposed toward Poles. One has the impression of having come to a different country. In the evening, in a nearby little wood, we lit a campfire and fried up scrambled eggs from 20 eggs, and rendered 1 kg of fatback into lard.

August 17, 1940, Saturday

Word is going around that the French have held Lt. Słomka along with the rest of the company as a labor company. In the afternoon, sunbathing in the mountains. I found a lot of people there sunbathing in Adam's costume. In the evening I visited the former Castle men.

August 18, 1940, Sunday

In the chapel located in one of the barracks a service was held. It was celebrated by a French priest, who delivered the sermon in French, while we sang Polish hymns. After the service Wolski and Długosz took photos of us (the Castle men). Word is going around that the Germans have launched an attack on England. In the afternoon I was in the small town of Aubagne with Janek Solec. and Józek Rej, at the photographer's and to listen to the radio.
