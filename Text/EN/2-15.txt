January 29, 1940, Monday
Nothing important, in good spirits.

January 30, 1940, Tuesday
In the evening Corporal Francuz takes me out for hot wine.

January 31, 1940, Wednesday
Preparations for departure.

February 1, 1940

At 5 o'clock Corporal Biliński woke me up, saying I was to go with him and Mr. Wolski to the station at Augan with the provisions. Before departure I receive 21 francs in pay. At 7:30 we are at the station in Augan. We find the station deserted, because the stationmaster - the one and only man who handles everything connected with running the station - had gone to a café. We finally find him. Units gradually stream in, and at 2:45 p.m. the transport departs via Guer and Rennes, Tours, Bourges, Lyon, to Orange station. The journey lasted 38 hours.
