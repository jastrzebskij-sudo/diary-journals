April 1, 1941, Tuesday, April
Wake-up is half an hour earlier now, that is at 6:30. A library has been given over for our use; there are quite a few Polish books here.

April 2, 1941, Tuesday
Mietek Grzegorzewski made a bet with Cierkosz for 20 francs that he would drink 3 liters of wine, not vomit, and after coming into the hall not say a word to anyone. But he lost the bet on both counts, and we got a lot of laughs out of him. Kuźniak was punished with detention because he refused to go to a lecture.

April 3, 1941, Wednesday
The radio news reports the death of the Hungarian foreign minister, Count Teleki, who committed suicide, the reason being that the Germans were forcing Hungary to move against Yugoslavia, with which Hungary had signed a treaty.

April 4, 1941, Thursday
Nothing worth noting.

April 5, 1941, Friday
Like a bolt from the blue comes the news that the Germans have taken Benghazi in Libya from the English.
