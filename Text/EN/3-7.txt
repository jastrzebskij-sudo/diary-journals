February 16, Sunday

Cierkosz arrived with the new group, and today, drunk, he already got into a row with Lt. Kluz. In the evening I had a beer with Zelek and Dziadkowiec, who had also just arrived.

February 17, Monday

The French inform us that on Thursday or Friday we will go to a camp, because the Italian-German commission demanded it. They also arrested one of the Poles who had too loose a tongue and somewhat communist views.

February 18, Tuesday

Word reaches us that the English bombed Kraków and the area around Katowice. Another group of about 25 men arrived, including Sgt. Gbur and Czarnota.

February 19, Wednesday

Again a new group arrived, also about 25 men, and 10 Poles, 4 Englishmen, and 1 Dutchman were released from custody, having been held there for about a month. The Englishmen and the Dutchman know how to sing the march "Polonia."
