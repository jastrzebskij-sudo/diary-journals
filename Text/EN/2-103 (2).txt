December 25, Wednesday. First Day of Christmas
The company received an invitation to the French recreation room for
the holiday. So a few of us, myself and Jasio included, went to see how
they celebrate the holiday.
The hall was decorated with French and Polish flags; in the corner
stood a small Christmas tree, with a table of gifts in front of it.
A speech was given by the camp commandant, and then the raffle
began. Everyone present had a number, which they received upon
entering, and when that number was called, they went up to the table
and received a very modest gift from the hostess.

December 26, 1940, Thursday
I spend the evening in the barrack, at the cinema.

December 27, 1940, Friday
Only in the afternoon do we work, clearing the yard in front of the
barracks; on this occasion we made a snowman.

December 28, 1940, Saturday
Rumors are circulating that the Germans are going to occupy the rest of
France. And that General de Gaulle has announced that the French who
are on his side should demonstrate this to the world by not going out
into the streets for one hour, starting at 12 noon on January 1, 1940.
