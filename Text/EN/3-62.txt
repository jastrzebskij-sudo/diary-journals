August 20, 1941, Wednesday
The shirt thieves, namely Walmus and Szymański Mieczysław, were punished by today's order with 15 days of strict arrest, of which 8 days in a cell. In the evening the gendarmes brought in legionnaires who had deserted from the Legion two days ago. They were placed under arrest right away.

August 21, 1941, Thursday
For July and August we were not issued soap, and because of this I haven't washed with soap for several days.

August 22, 1941, Friday
Nothing special.

August 23, 1941, Saturday
At 10 in the morning a medal ceremony took place for 5 people; among them is one Pole who has served 15 years in the Legion. Poles were also present during the decoration ceremony, after which a parade took place.

(Clipping pasted in from a French newspaper)
Berlin presents a new tally of the most murderous fighting in military and humanitarian history. According to the Wehrmacht, Russian losses have reached 5 million killed, yet 140 Soviet divisions remain in the line.

August 24, 1941, Sunday
The choir did not sing at Mass because Szpotnar has no time to rehearse since he plays in the orchestra. In the afternoon Poles and legionnaires played a match against the Arabs, whose goalkeeper was from Saïda's first team, and won 3–7. In the evening the garrison orchestra performed in town, and because of this roll call was at 22:00.

August 25, 1941, Monday
We received the 3rd injection and turned in our mattresses, and in exchange got straw pallets. We were also issued soap coupons, and finally there is something to wash with. On Saturday the Red Cross gave us 10 dkg of soap each.
London radio reports the entry of English and Soviet troops into Iran (Persia).

August 26, 1941, Tuesday
We saw the Arabs eating young leaves, or fruit of the cactus — I don't know what they're properly called. This fruit has a sweet taste but is dangerous, because as self-defense it has countless tiny little spines which, on contact, stay in your hand and are hard to get rid of. Many of our fellows caused themselves pain with it, but I found a way around it. Namely, I watched how the Arabs do it, and they do it in such a way that before picking the fruit they knock all the spines off it with branches. I tried it that way and it worked well.
