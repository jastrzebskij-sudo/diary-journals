September 1, Sunday
Anniversary of the outbreak of the war. On this occasion there was a service in the
Dominican church, at which, however, I was not present, because I had gone with Tadek
Mazur to Carpiagne. When I walked into the barrack I felt just
as if I'd come home on leave. All our own fellows there. There
we fill out demobilization forms and return to Marseille. At the hospital
we meet Sergeant Komisarz. He treats us to strong beer and we spend
a pleasant evening to the sound of Mazur's guitar and the guitar of one
of the hospital's residents.
(photo)
Tadeusz Mazur, September 3, 1940

September 2, Monday
We return to Carpiagne because the departure fell through. Lt. Pasionek also goes
to the camp. At the Company the captain makes difficulties about
taking us back into the company, since we had been absent for several days. Finally he
softened and said that maybe something could be arranged.
It was only in the evening that Opałko reminded me that it's my name day.
