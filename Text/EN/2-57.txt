July 15, Monday
The sea is very rough.

July 16, Tuesday
Corporal Sadecki brings me a suitcase from Perpignan; the suitcase, shirt, and tie together cost 100 francs. In the evening, while bathing in the river, I lost my little gold cross, but after a long search I found it.

July 17, Wednesday
Black dances and a drunk French soldier liven up our evening. After the evening news, I was with Corporal Jan Sadecki at a café having beer, and there the plan arose to go to Perpignan

July 18, 1940, Thursday
I went with Corporal Sadecki to Perpignan to go shopping. I bought light-colored trousers and a shirt of a similar color, plus a tie, for 38 fr., a windbreaker for 69 fr., a small case for 5 fr., and I also visited the Hotel Ambasador — in total I spent 400 francs.
I spend the beautiful moonlit evening, as usual, on a walk.

July 19, 1940, Friday
