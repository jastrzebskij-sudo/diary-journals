January 5, 1940, Friday

In the evening we throw ourselves a little drinking party. The guests are Corporal Kowalski and Mr. Iwański, commonly known as "Spirytusik" (Little Spirit). Vodka is not allowed to be sold, but nevertheless Spirytusik managed to get hold of 2 bottles of cognac at 10:30 p.m. Sagański spent the night at my place.

January 6, 1940, Saturday - Epiphany (Three Kings' Day)
