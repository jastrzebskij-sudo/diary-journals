Around 1 p.m. we set off again, one by one so as to be safer from air raids, with the assembly point designated at the barracks of the Armored Battalion in Lublin.

I arrived first; after some time a few more vehicles joined. By 8 p.m. none of the officers had arrived, and we had no fuel. After a conference we drove onto the highway toward Chełm. We spent the night on the highway. Toward morning the Park Column of the 2nd Battalion passed by, under the command of Captain Ciągliński, and they gave us fuel; I joined this column because I saw order here. Traffic on the highway was so heavy it was hard to drive in a column. In Chełm we learned that a motor column was standing in the forest beyond the town, so we drove to join it. Just as we reached it, German planes swept in and bombed the railway stations where our column had stood five minutes earlier. We drove on toward Łuck; nothing important happened along the way.
