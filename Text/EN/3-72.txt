September 29, 1941, Monday
Among those who arrived yesterday is one very good tenor. My friends taught me to play poker, and I kill all my free moments with it now. A new garrison commandant arrived; he is a French captain.

September 30, 1941, Tuesday
October 1, 1941, Wednesday
Wiktor Kosiński no longer went to the casino today because our officers now dine at the hotel, and only 6 people remain at the casino, so one is enough. On the staff there is a legionnaire.

October 2, 1941, Thursday
Somehow nothing new is happening. We have more peace and quiet here than in the barracks.

October 3, 1941, Friday
Many legionnaires have joined the garrison orchestra, and because of this our men were removed from it.

October 4, 1941, Saturday
I'm having a very unlucky day at cards; playing just for 10 centimes I lost about 30 fr.

October 5, 1941, Sunday
I was at High Mass. The choir did not sing because the French were singing, and the company goes to church individually. In the afternoon a football match took place at the municipal stadium between Saïda and Oran (sailors); Saïda lost 3–1. I watched the match from the green stand because entry costs 8 fr. 50 cm., which is expensive for our pockets.

October 6, 1941, Monday
They don't exchange our sleeping bags for us the way they did with the sheets, so we have to wash them ourselves, and washing one is a nasty job and uses a lot of soap. That's just what I did today.

October 7, 1941, Tuesday
The new garrison commandant carried out an inspection of our barracks. Everyone living in them stood at assembly. The captain asked the barracks commander, Mr. G., what we do and whether we have only these one set of overalls. And he answered that he had offered us more but we didn't want to accept it. The captain reportedly said that tomorrow a colonel, a friend of the Poles, would arrive and it would somehow get sorted out. After the inspection I went for a walk. We now have a second spring. All the plants scorched by the summer sun have come back to life, and there are many flowers. I brought Wiktor a nice little bouquet from that walk. Radio reports that the Germans have abandoned Leningrad, directing their offensive at Moscow.
