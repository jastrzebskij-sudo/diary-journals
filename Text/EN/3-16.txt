March 20, 1941, Thursday
Aspirant Jałowy, the one who deserted from the company to Oran, has a bandaged head, and I heard him say himself that blood poisoning had set in. He supposedly got the wound from being hit by a car while drunk, at least that's what everyone claims. He came back from Oran and was put in the guardhouse for a few days. Aspirant Złotnicki was with him.
In the afternoon we were issued underwear, two sets each, and boots — though I have no boots for now. In the evening 18 Poles arrived from Oran, among them Jezierski Kaziu and Laszkiewicz Stefan, and 2 officers who apparently gave up their pay and the officers' camp to come to our camp.

March 21, 1941, Friday
Lice were found in the underwear issued yesterday, so the drawers were collected again for disinfection. The shirts were new, so they were clean.

March 22, 1941, Saturday
All day we had work shelling peas for tomorrow. Our platoon is on duty.

March 23, 1941, Sunday
Only half the men turn out for the daily roll call, but almost everyone goes to church, and they show discipline, which makes good propaganda for Poland.
