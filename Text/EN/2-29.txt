Michał, and mjst Haze from the 3rd Battalion.

Sunday, March 31, 1940
Sr. S/Sgt. Kurnik hosts me and Wolski, along with a sergeant, a friend of his. I got thoroughly drunk.

Monday, April 1, 1940
Sgt. Wolski was assigned to the 1st Company of the 2nd Battalion.

Tuesday, April 2, 1940
I receive back pay and current pay, totaling 1337 francs.

Wednesday, April 6, 1940
The Germans have occupied Denmark and attacked Norway.

Thursday, April 11, 1940
First live-fire range in the mountains near Lagarde Paol.

Sunday, April 14, 1940
Yesterday, while bathing in Orange, I bought myself a gold chain and cross, and Cpl. Nocoń a silver one. On Sunday we throw ourselves a little party with wine and cakes, and in the afternoon the opening of the community room — in an old wine press. A very dull program, songs and recitations. In the evening a radio bulletin from Toulouse reports the sinking of 7 German destroyers in the Narvik fjord.
