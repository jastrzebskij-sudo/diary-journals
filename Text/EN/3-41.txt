June 8, 1941, Sunday

Service at 7 in the morning, but it was almost shameful to go to church because the choir did not sing, only Soboszczyk fumbled about on the harmonium and began singing a few hymns, but the company did not sing along. Another group has managed to slip away from Marseille, there are five of them, among them Mietek Szymański. He recounts that yet another group was caught in Oran by the French and no one knows what happened to them. And that the story about Lt. Straub having stolen company funds is a lie. Sadecki and Wolski ended up in some kind of shelter for invalids or the elderly.

June 9, 1941, Monday

News reaches us that yesterday the English attacked Syria and that they have already taken Damascus and Beirut.

June 10, 1941, Tuesday

It turns out that the news from Syria was not so favorable, because those two aforementioned cities have not yet been taken.

June 11, 1941, Wednesday

And again five more arrived from Marseille; I know one of them, namely Rutkowski. He recounts that it is very hard to get through because the French police catch people and take them to a penal camp, but the brotherhood flees anyway however it can; he was traveling in another group but the whole group, except for him, was caught, and he arrived in Oran without a penny. Without money because it was held by the group's leader, who was caught.
