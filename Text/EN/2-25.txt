Sunday, March 17, 1940 — Palm Sunday

A very beautiful service, during which one of the officers played the violin beautifully. After the service, the first briefing with the new company commander. After lunch I'm on duty as commander of the garrison guard. This is the first guard duty on French soil. It was made eventful by the arrest of Cpl. Maczewski Edward for breaking a comrade's leg while drunk. The arrest protected him from being beaten by the comrades who stood up in defense of the injured man. The second man arrested, for drunkenness, was a Pole, a corporal who had come from Romania, but the duty officer had him released.

Monday, March 18, 1940

I rest after finishing my duty. The company returns from tank exercises full of enthusiasm. I learned that my tank has been given to another company.

Tuesday, March 19, 1940

A memorial service for the soul of the late Marshal Piłsudski, and normal duties.

Wednesday, March 20, 1940 — The British bomb Germany.

Thursday, March 21, 1940 — Nothing important.

Friday, March 22, 1940 — Nothing important.
