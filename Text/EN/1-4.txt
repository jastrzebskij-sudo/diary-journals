The entire Armored Battalion left Łuck for the forest and joined our column, forming an Armored Corps center. We sat in the forest for a few days; no one was allowed to drive vehicles or walk along the firebreaks. Only walking through the forest itself was allowed. No one was permitted to go far from the forest. After great effort I nevertheless obtained permission to go into Łuck for supplies. In town you could not get bread. You could get sausage, but you had to search for hours, because whatever the butcher prepared was snapped up at once, and then you had to wait for a new batch.

One evening we received orders to move out. The night was dark — you couldn't see three steps ahead — and no lights were allowed. As we pulled onto the highway, my jeep's engine stalled and the whole column drove off without me. I struggled with the vehicle alone, since no one would help me; luckily I had bought a pocket flashlight in Łuck the day before. At that point several local residents came along and pushed me, and the engine started. I caught up with the column and we drove along country
