May 19, 1941, Monday
Kula sits in the guardhouse for a second day. Some French general made an inspection
of the garrison but did not enter our area, and the company went out for a walk.

May 20, 1941, Tuesday

For the first time we bathe in the pool, it is very good. The pool
has been made available for our use on Tuesdays and Fridays before noon.

May 21, 1941, Wednesday

Our choir and the sports group departed for Maskara for a sports
festival.

May 22, 1941, Thursday

The weather is nasty, cold and rainy, and so the sports festival in
Saida and Maskara was postponed to Sunday, and those who went to Maskara
are to return on Monday. I was on the "sixteen" [duty shift]. 
The radio reports that for several days fighting has been going on on Crete and that the Germans
are achieving successes.

May 23, 1941, Friday

The radio reports that the English sank 30 German-Italian ships
that were sailing to Crete.

May 24, 1941, Saturday

The radio again reports the sinking of the English battleship Hood
by the German battleship Bismarck.
