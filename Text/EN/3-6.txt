February 11, 1941, Tuesday

A new group arrived from Marseille, about 10 men; among them is Karwaciński.

February 12, 1941, Wednesday

Boredom. I don't want to go into town because I have no money, and just wandering the streets is something I really dislike. In the evening I only went with Fatyga for dinner at a second-rate little restaurant.

February 13, 1941, Thursday

The group that included Kuźmiak was detained in Morocco and brought to Oran; they are with us now. One of the Englishmen has disappeared.

February 14 – Friday
Nothing of importance.

February 15, Saturday

In the evening 29 Poles arrived by ship from Marseille, whom the police had arrested in the port and brought to us.

This makes me think that this is already being done in agreement with the French, because they expect the rest of France to be occupied by the Germans. One man from this group was suspected of espionage by some Frenchman, who claimed to have seen him in Lyon in a German uniform. He was arrested.
