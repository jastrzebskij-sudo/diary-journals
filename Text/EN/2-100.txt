December 16, Monday
80 men from our company, myself and Jasio among them, were
temporarily detached to the barracks of the 43 p.p.f. to repair roads and
yards. We set out there very reluctantly, but along the way our mood
improved, and we entered the barracks singing. The Frenchman
assigned to lead us (the bespectacled one) was very pleased with this,
which he expressed by telling the chief how the company had lined up,
and how the French officers kept asking who these people were, i.e.
"qu'est-ce que c'est — Allemands?" [what is this — Germans?], making
comical faces as he said it. One must understand that the French are
very afraid of the Germans.
On the second day of work, Sadowski and Komar sneaked off to Ste
Marthe, and we grumbled very loudly against this. Jasio Sadecki
needlessly made a scene about it with Sadowski. In the company,
certain individuals accuse Lt. Sroub of embezzling company funds. But
at the company assembly, all of this turned out to be gossip. Lt. Sroub,
