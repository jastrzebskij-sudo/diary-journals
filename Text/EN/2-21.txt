Łowczowski, and to Grześkowiak and Irenka C.

The weather, yesterday and today, is very nice and warm. I lent Cpl. Sułek 30 francs, since he's going on leave and has no money for anything.

Thursday, February 22, 1940

In the evening I went to see Sgt. Świtajewski but didn't find him in, as he had gone on leave. Then I went to the cinema, where they were showing a film with Marta Eggerth — in Poland, I believe, it was shown under the title "The Nightingale of Vienna" — along with a very silly French comedy and a P.A.T. newsreel.

Friday, February 23, 1940

I buy myself a pocket flashlight, paying 18 francs.

Saturday, February 24, 1940

At 1 p.m. we're supposed to leave for Serignan or Orange to bathe, but we're late, and the bus pulls away right from under our noses. We set off on foot, and along the way we try hitchhiking; one car picked up three of the men, who had our laundry with them, while Cpl. Dierzyk and I go on foot. For the first time this year I saw a tree in bloom.

In Serignan I bathed under the shower, while Cpl. Dierzyk went on to Orange, since he didn't want to bathe there; afterward I had to put the same clothes back on, since his and my laundry were with the ones who had gone to Orange.
