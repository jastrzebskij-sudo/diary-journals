After the service the major, the senior Polish commander in Africa, held a briefing with the company on the subject of Friday's incident, and said he would try to have the culprits removed from our ranks so as not to spoil our reputation. Afterward there was an Easter blessing-of-food gathering, attended by the garrison commander, Maj. Fuzeau, with his wife and daughter, the parish priest, two French officers, and Polish legionnaires and several others of various nationalities. For the holiday, officers from Mascara (Poles) also came to visit us. The dining hall was beautifully decorated with Polish and French flags, and in the middle a large, beautiful eagle. There were many wildflowers on the tables. Lt. Piątkowski was the first to speak. He thanked everyone for coming and offered his wishes. The garrison commander replied in French. The Easter meal itself was very tasty but not overly abundant, so that we finished long before the guests did and began entertaining them with song. We sang the anthems of both nations and several Polish songs. One of our comrades, who knows a little French and was somewhat tipsy, launched into a speech, spouting terrible nonsense, saying among other things: "Nous avons jednego gen. Sikorskiego, Wous avez jednego marszałka Petaina" (we have one General Sikorski, you have one Marshal Petain). His comrades laughed about him all day. In closing, Lt. Raczyński thanked the parish priest for his cooperation with the Poles.

Satisfaction was visible on the guests' faces as they left the gathering.
