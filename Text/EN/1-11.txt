I left Paris around 2 in the afternoon for Rennes and from there to Guer. And there a sergeant of the Polish Army already drove us to Coëtquidan. I was temporarily quartered in barrack B 15, from where I was assigned to the billeting platoon. After a two-week stay I was appointed by Lieutenant Colonel Hofbauer as commandant of transit barrack B 15. There I took Bombardier Józef Walczak, a very honest man, as my assistant.

From the Castle unit I am the first, with number 1262. I borrowed a passport from a gentleman I did not know at all, named Mr. Sumyk, and exchanged cash. After some time the transit quarters were moved to building C 1, where I am commandant of half the building. The next to arrive from the Castle unit were Władysław Gołębiowski and Dominik Szpak. After their discharge they had worked at the P.Z.L. plant in Mielec. I took them on as assistants and they were with me for about 3 weeks, but they were not suited to such duties and I sent them off to the 4th Infantry Regiment.
