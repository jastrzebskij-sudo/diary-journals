February 23, 1941, Sunday

Quite early we go to the service in fours, at which we do not sing. Word reaches us that the English have landed at Ostend and that Marshal Śmigły-Rydz has taken command of the Polish army in Egypt. We spend the rest of the day wandering around Saïda.

February 24, 1941, Monday

We begin the day with prayer and exercise. The company is already fairly well coordinated.
(2 photos) Zarychta Wincenty; Grzegorzewski Mieczysław

February 25, 1941, Tuesday

On returning from morning exercise we put on a parade for the garrison commander, with which he was very pleased. Major Brzozowski arrived, bringing us a radio and a chess set, and informed us about the political situation and about our task. In the evening the Jewish men who are in the labor company put on a concert for us. It turned out fairly well. Yesterday several men were punished for returning late from town; among them was Dzikowski, who behaved rudely toward the company commander and was therefore punished with detention. He refused to go, but under pressure from the company he went. For now, internal relations are better than in Ste Marthe.
