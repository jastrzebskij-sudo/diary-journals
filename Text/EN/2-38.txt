May 21, 1940, Tuesday
Outpost exercises. By the captain's order I am the outpost commander, and the captain observed me because he wanted to get to know me. The exercise turned out well overall.

May 22, 1940, Wednesday
The company digs anti-aircraft trenches.

May 23, 1940, Thursday
The part of the company that had already been vaccinated against typhus during my leave received the second dose. In the evening, news about the halting of the German offensive and the recapture of the city of Arras. Dzierzyk still hasn't returned.

May 24, 1940, Friday
We're loafing about alongside those who were vaccinated.

May 25, 1940, Saturday
I am transferred to the 2nd platoon and in connection with this I change quarters. As a farewell to the old quarters we arrange a little scrambled-egg feast. I make the new quarters nice for myself.
