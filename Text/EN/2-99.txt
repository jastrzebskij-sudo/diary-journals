Last night I had a very strange dream. I dreamed that I was at some
church service where everyone present was lying on bunks or
stretchers. Among those kneeling I remember only Mich. Wolski,
because he was lying next to me. Then the two of us, "Kić" and I, were
walking down some road, and suddenly we hear what sounds like the
drone of an airplane. We look to the side, and there stand two
machine guns, so we run to them to open fire, and in the distance the
silhouette of the plane is visible. But this plane turns into a cross and
falls at my feet, shattering, yet all its pieces are still held together by
Christ, who is upon it. I lifted the cross from the ground, when suddenly
some bird flies down over me — something like a falcon, something
like an eagle, not very large and not entirely white — and grabs hold of
the cross and my hands and carries me upward. Then I fall into some
kind of faint; the whole world grows misty before me, and I have the
feeling that death is approaching. I begin to pray very fervently that I do
not want to leave the world yet, and I am very afraid to leave, because I
have so many sins and it has been so long since I went to confession.
And, strange to say, I feel myself sinking toward the earth, and at last I
stood on it fully conscious, and the bird and the cross vanished, and Lt.
Strączek comes up to me and says: "Why, you fool, don't you want to
go to heaven alive?" And I tell him that I would go to heaven, but I did
not know where it was.
Waking up, for a long moment I could not realize that I was lying on a
bunk. And below, Józio was snoring (for I had been sleeping on the
upper bunk).
