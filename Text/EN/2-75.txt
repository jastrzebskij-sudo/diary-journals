September 7, 1940, Saturday
The company receives a radio, through the efforts of the company commander, Capt.
Hucakowski, given as a gift by a friend of his, some Major (I don't know which one). Apparently
General Duch was in the camp yesterday and reportedly declared that we would
receive the demobilization bonus before long. In the afternoon the company was called
to demobilize, but the riflemen refused to sign the
demobilization card without the bonus being paid out. So the demobilization
was postponed.
