December 25 - I am busy with performances all afternoon; at 10 a.m. a transport arrived from Romania, among whom was a passing acquaintance from Bucharest, I believe a doctor from the State Forests [administration].

December 26 - Second day of the holiday, we are working.

December 27 - Nothing important.

December 28 - ditto.

December 29 - Corporal Walczak returns from leave, and on this occasion there is a feast well-lubricated with alcohol, attended by the sergeant major, Warrant Officer Czapiński, and Sergeant Pieszczuchowicz. Sergeant Winogradzki was removed from his post over some unexplained matter.

December 30 - Warrant Officer Grzyb, Staff Sergeant Aleksander Sagański, Corporal Tadeusz Zegarłowski, and Corporal Miklas arrived at the camp. Sagański and Zegarłowski went with me right away to Comblessac to see Lieutenant Gulin, whom we caught just as he was setting off for Guer; along the way we talked about all our acquaintances. On the way we stocked up on wine, sprats, and sugar. Zegarłowski and Sagański slept at my place. Warrant Officer Grzyb stayed with a colleague, and Miklas in the general hall.
