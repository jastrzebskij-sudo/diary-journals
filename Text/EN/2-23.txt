I bruised on that outing hurts a great deal and has swollen up. In the evening, night exercises. The men carry them out quite willingly, regarding them more as entertainment.

Tuesday, February 27, 1940

I take up my first duty as company duty NCO in France, after a 5-month break.

Wednesday, February 28, 1940

The duty private overslept and woke me half an hour late, that is, at 7. Even so, the company made it to assembly on time.

Thursday, February 29, 1940

Nothing to note.

Friday, March 1, 1940

During drills I lost my watch, but it was found by one of the privates. We draw our pay, totaling 452 francs; I set aside 20 of it for a shared supper. Everyone billeted together plays a card game called "a thousand."

Saturday, March 2, 1940

I bought myself a pocketknife, paying 10 francs.
