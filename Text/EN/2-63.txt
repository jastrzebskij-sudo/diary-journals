Of course I set the matter straight, though he did not believe it, and said that he would report this to the authorities at the appropriate moment, along with the fact that in Roan I had not carried out his order and had not allowed my men to bring coffee for his crew. My crew did not want coffee because there was no time to drink it.

July 30, 1940, Tuesday

First English lesson; I'm also learning French. Janek Sadecki went to the port of Vandres, to a villa near Kotani.

July 31, 1940, Wednesday

Studying English and French.

August 1, 1940, Thursday

Same as the previous day.

August 2, 1940, Friday

The company switched to independent self-catering, and the professional soldiers must pay for their food. They are to pay two weeks in advance and two weeks in arrears. The food improved a little in quality, but there isn't enough of it. Now provisions are drawn from the military quartermaster's office, rather than, as before, from the Spanish camp.
