December 19, 1939, Tuesday

I received a package from one of the conscripts, from the Polish Women's Circle, from Mrs. Marja Wojciechowska.

December 20, 21, 22, 1939. Nothing important to note. We go to rehearsals at the cinema. Pawłowski and Matyjasek left for Angers.

December 23, 1939 - Christmas Eve

We perform the nativity play (jasełka) at the officers' casino in the presence of President Raczkiewicz, General Sikorski, and members of the government; we gave them a surprise.

Afterward we performed it again in the Administrative Company at the Assembly Station, and then had Christmas Eve supper standing in the recreation-room tearoom.

I have never had a Christmas Eve like this one - there were a few tins of sprats, potatoes, fish, and tea. It was a fine sight to see a colleague, dressed as the King of England, eating fish while holding it in one hand and a piece of bread in the other.

After the meal - for it could hardly be called a proper Christmas Eve supper - we went to the officer cadets' casino and performed the nativity play there. At 9 o'clock I was asleep.
