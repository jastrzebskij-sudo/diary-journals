April 25, 1941, Friday

A day of mourning on account of the death of the French general, commander of the Foreign Legion. The flag is raised at half-mast, and the garrison commander, after raising it, read out the deceased's biography and merits. Rain fell during this ceremony.

April 26, 1941, Saturday

As a result of telegrams sent to Algiers saying the company was on the verge of falling apart, a Red Cross delegate arrived today, familiarized himself with the situation, and promised a pay raise. Apparently pay is now to be according to military rank. There is discontent in the company over this.

April 27, 1941, Sunday

The Red Cross delegate was present at Mass and was very moved by the Polish character of the service. As a reward he allotted 15 francs each to the choir members. The afternoon was marked by a terrible thrashing of our footballers, losing 6–1 to Saïda. Even so, the play was fine.

April 28, 1941, Monday

In the afternoon there was a practice parade for the legion's anniversary celebrations in Saïda. Poles also took part in it; the garrison commander was pleased with us.
