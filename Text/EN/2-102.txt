After dinner we all moved to Ste Marthe, since that's where we are to
celebrate the holiday. Jasio immediately went to bring holiday greetings
to the Greek girls, and when he came back he said they were very
pleased and surprised.
Then we offered our greetings to our French acquaintances, and one of
the Greek girls, the elder Marja, came and thanked us for the greetings.
Before leaving we conveyed to her, for the Greek nation, our
congratulations on the victory in Albania. The girl was so moved by this
that she burst into tears. We were invited to these French people's place
for 8 o'clock.
Christmas Eve in our company was splendidly organized by Lt. Straub,
with the help of our French commanders. Even the commandant of the
whole camp helped a great deal. Combined with the Christmas Eve
celebration was the opening of the recreation room, which was
excellently organized and decorated, considering the conditions we find
ourselves in.
General Kleberg honored this celebration with his presence, along with
his wife, as did several French officers, headed by the camp
commandant. Also invited was the camp staff, such as the hostesses
from the French recreation room, the head cook, and various overseers.
In the run-up to Christmas Eve, the wife of our French company
commander showed lively activity aimed at making it more pleasant.
The celebration was opened with a speech by Gen. Kleberg, who
thanked the organizers in both Polish and French, and then delivered a
rousing patriotic speech to us. He recalled the past, and about the future
