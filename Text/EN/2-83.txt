September 26, 1940, Thursday
Misiek W., Janek S., Michał Bardzki and I went on an excursion to the
highest peak, taking a few photographs there. In a beautiful ravine
we found a scorpion, whose sting causes terrible suffering.
We burned it in a fire. A French captain has been assigned to the company and
today he held a briefing with our officers.

September 27, 1940, Friday
The four of us went to Marseille for vehicles. Misiek W. and Janek S.
were issued a truck and a field kitchen, while Józek R. and I got
an old Ford which we could not get started, so it went in for repair.
With us were French non-commissioned officers, very likeable and
keen supporters of General de Gaulle. We had a bit of a drink with them.
The only newspaper published in Lyon in the Polish language writes in a
pro-German spirit, but jokingly gives one to understand that one shouldn't
believe everything it writes. As an example I am pasting in a clipping from this
newspaper.
