Stefan's Diary

I begin this diary with a summary of past events, starting from the moment war broke out, that is, from September 1, 1939. On that historic day, being at Fort Wola for supplies, I was bombed; several bombs fell very close by. After the air raid, which took place around 3 p.m., many people still did not believe it was war. The next few days passed under the threat of continual bombardment.

On September 6 I left Warsaw, evacuating the remaining equipment of the Supply Company of the 3rd Armored Battalion. The column, under the command of Lieutenant Jaroński, waited for the rest of the Battalion at Saska Kępa. Around 11 o'clock the rest arrived and we set off for Lublin via Otwock. We drove without lights; fortunately the night was moonlit. The column drove in complete order along the Vistula embankment, but once it reached the Warsaw–Lublin highway, where there was enormous congestion, the column fell apart. Near Garwolin I met several vehicles from our column, but none of our officers were among them. We stood for an hour, then drove on; past Ryki we were attacked by German aircraft and took cover
