café, he scolded us for having suitcases. "And yet he had the biggest
one of all."
In the evening the sailors led us to the ship, but they did not allow us to
take our suitcases, saying they would carry them themselves.
We had to climb up onto the ship on a rope, since there was a guard
posted at the entrance. They loaded us into some kind of storeroom, and
there we sat for 24 hours without light, food, or drink.
Only afterward, once the ship had sailed out of the port, did one of the
sailors bring us food and drink and open a small window, which gave
us a little light and also served as a latrine, since there was no other
way out.
The whole journey, which we spent sitting in the storeroom, lasted 3
days. The ship sailed along the Spanish coast toward Oran. When we
caught sight of the shores of Africa, the sailor looking after us told us to
hide under a shelf and surrounded us with cork life belts. It was
extremely cramped there, and we sat like that for a couple of hours,
because an Italian commission was inspecting the ship in the port.
