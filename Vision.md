# Vision: the finished series (living document)

Source: Jim, 2026-11-07. Purpose: Christmas 2026 gift (soft 2026-12-15, hard 2026-12-24). Tone: documentary, but sentimental.

## Look and sound
- Greyscale and sepia throughout, light film artifacting (grain, flicker) so it feels like WWII-era film; matching period treatment on the voice audio.
- Same visual design for the Polish and English versions; closed captions in the spoken language, selectable in YouTube.
- Journal on screen: Jim wants the feel of the handwritten journal; it need not be readable, since the text is spoken. **Decided: the on-screen journal is the scans of the original handwritten notebooks** (Images\Notebooks\1 and \2; entries are dated, which joins each scan to the text). The spoken text comes from the typed transcription (Images\, "Spisane z dwóch zeszytów", transcribed by Jim's uncle).
- **Archival footage and imagery predominate.** Grandfather's face is used sparingly: episode intro and outro, an occasional fade-in mid-episode; present, not in the spotlight (Jim, 2026-11-07).

## Episode structure
1. Fade in from black. One region shows the journal title page; Episode 1 carries the series title, later episodes "continued" plus their date range.
2. A moment later the other region fades in on grandfather's face, which begins speaking the journal text (lip-synced).
3. The journal page pans top to bottom as it is read.
4. **Landmarks** (events or places with imagery): a region fades to a panned crop of the chosen image, or a short video clip. Landmark sides are used equally but organically, not strict left-right alternation.
5. Layout is a **moving division**, not a fixed 50/50: a strong landmark image may dominate the frame.
6. **Major events:** both regions fade out and the landmark takes the full frame. Archival audio or suitable music plays as a break between voiceover segments, giving time to reflect.
7. Audio and landmark fade to black briefly; journal and grandfather return and continue.

## Release plan (Jim, 2026-11-07)
- Christmas premiere: Episodes 1-4 (Sep 1939 - Jun 1940), PL and EN, ending on the Fall of France as a cliffhanger.
- Episodes 5-16 weekly from early January 2027 (scheduled in YouTube).
- Notebook 2 (Feb - ~Dec 1942) was never transcribed: transcribe and proofread in January, then continue the weekly series from about April.
- The finale below closes the **last notebook 2 episode**, not Episode 16.

## Finale (last episode of the whole series)
- Over the last section, the Polish national anthem fades in behind grandfather's voice.
- As the voiceover ends, the title page returns beside his face, as in the opening. Both fade as the anthem ends.
- Moment of silence, then a Chopin nocturne over a slow-panning slideshow of the family: grandparents, their children and spouses, their children and spouses, up to the present. The slideshow moves gradually from greyscale/sepia to full colour.
