## Notable-page selection, second pass

The first pass (last turn) only caught pages that were *unusually long*, which happened to cluster
almost entirely in Book 3 (the Algeria/Sahara labor-camp period, 1941–42). Re-scanning **all 195
pages** for historically notable content — regardless of length — surfaced a much richer, better
distributed set, especially in Books 1 and 2:

- **Book 1** (Sep–Dec 1939): the Luftwaffe bombing of Warsaw, the retreat across Poland, encountering
  the invading Soviet column near Czortków on Sept 17, the border crossing into Romania, and the
  journey through Bucharest, Trieste, Venice, and wartime Paris to reach the Polish Army's camp in
  France — is the most visually rewarding stretch in the entire diary and was almost entirely
  missed by the length filter, since most of these entries are terse.
- **Book 2** (Dec 1939 – Oct 1940): includes President Raczkiewicz and General Sikorski attending a
  camp nativity play at Christmas 1939, a memorial service for Marshal Piłsudski, an eyewitness
  account of the German invasion of the Low Countries (May 10, 1940) and the evacuation of Paris,
  the retreat and collapse of France under Italian air attack, and radio reports of the Hitler–Franco
  meeting at Hendaye.
- **Book 3** (1941–42) still contributes strong material — Barbarossa, Paderewski's death, the
  Sikorski-Mayski Agreement, the Sahara labor camp, and the January 1942 hunger strike — but is no
  longer the *only* source of imagery-worthy pages.

Episodes 2, 5, and 6 came up with no strong "notable event" candidate — those stretches are mostly
routine garrison/camp-life entries (choir rehearsals, football matches, minor personal incidents).
That's a legitimate finding, not a gap to force-fill: those episodes can rely on the diary page
images alone rather than a manufactured cutaway. Episode 8 initially came up empty too, but a closer
read (rather than a keyword scan) turned up three candidates: Greece's counteroffensive against
Italy, the opening of Britain's Egypt offensive (Operation Compass), and the company's own sea
crossing to Oran/Algiers — the transition into the North African setting that dominates the rest of
the series.

## Visual concept: dual imagery

At 20-30 minutes per episode, a single cutaway per notable page is too sparse — episodes need more
archival images than originally planned, and the intended on-screen treatment is a **combined
shot**: the diary page scan and a relevant period photograph displayed together (e.g. side by side,
or diary page with a smaller inset), rather than cutting away from the diary page entirely. The
diary page stays the visual anchor throughout; the archival photo supplements it rather than
replacing it. This affects the eventual video-assembly template (a two-image layout, not a single
full-frame slide) but doesn't change the storyboard research process itself — still one entry per
notable page, just with the expectation that most 20-30 min episodes should carry more than 2-3
archival images by the end of sourcing.

## Archival image sourcing, round 2

The Polish National Digital Archive (Narodowe Archiwum Cyfrowe, searchable via
szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl) substantially outperformed NARA for this diary's actual subject matter —
better hit rate, higher relevance, and clearer open-reuse licensing on many items. See
`Archival-Images/manifest.csv` for the full, growing list of sourced images with citations.

## Archival image sourcing, round 3 - equipment, insignia, and general context

In addition to page-specific images (filed under each episode's `archival-images/` folder),
`Archival-Images/General/` now holds equipment/uniform/insignia reference images that are relevant
across multiple episodes rather than tied to one diary page - e.g. German Panzer I/II tanks and a
motorized column from the 1940 Western campaign, a 1940 British War Office identification plate for
Soviet Red Army uniforms, and Free French Foreign Legion soldiers in the North African desert. Draw
from this folder when an episode needs a generic "what did the equipment/uniforms of this army look
like" cutaway that isn't anchored to a specific event. All entries are logged in
`Archival-Images/manifest.csv` with the episode field "General (equipment/context library - not
page-specific)".
