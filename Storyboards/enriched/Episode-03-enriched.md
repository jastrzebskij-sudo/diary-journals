## 1. Summary
Late February to mid-May 1940, Polish 2nd Division camp at Ste Cecile (Provence): drills, new officers, the issue of armoured equipment (he becomes a tank driver), Piłsudski memorial, Easter święcone and two mountain outings with French friends. Then his first leave: a muddled journey north to Pecquencourt, Irenka, English soldiers, first kiss. The leave is interrupted by the German attack of 10 May, seen as an air battle overhead; he travels back through alarms. Mood: warm, homesick, light, with dread arriving only in the last pages (assumption: this contrast is the point of the title).
