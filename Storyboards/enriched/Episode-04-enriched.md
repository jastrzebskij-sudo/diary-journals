## 1. Summary
17 May - 2 July 1940. Stefan's unit idles in southern France, entrains north to Versailles (29 May) as France collapses, then flees south ahead of the Germans, ending at Port Vendres with no ship left and into a gendarmerie-run camp on the beach. Mood: dull routine and drinking, then confusion, comic-bitter improvisation, boredom and demoralisation, ending in rumours of a wider war.
