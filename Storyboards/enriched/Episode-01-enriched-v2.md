## 1. Summary
Stefan, a supply-company driver in the 3rd Armored Battalion, is bombed at Fort Wola on 1 Sep 1939, leaves Warsaw on 6 Sep and retreats east through Lublin, Chelm, Luck and Tarnopol. On 17 Sep his column meets Soviet tanks near Czortkow and he crosses into Romania in tears. He works as a driver in Bucharest, enrolls in the Polish Army in France and travels via Yugoslavia and Italy to Paris and the Coetquidan camp. Mood: urgent and disoriented at first, then grief at the border, relief and small kindnesses, ending in barracks routine, the nativity play (Dec 1939) and New Year's Eve at camp ("We go to sleep at 9:30.").
