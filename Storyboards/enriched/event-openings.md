# event-openings: which episodes open with a major world event

Method: pages 1-2 of each episode were read from Text\EN. Evidence for any later page comes only from the storyboard notable-page tables (those pages were not read, per the prompt). An event qualifies if it is widely known and either sits in the first two pages or is the clear subject (title, notable pages). One-line news items on early pages do not qualify unless the event is widely known.

**Ambiguous:**

3. Only pages 1-2 were read. Later-page evidence (2-35, 2-91, 3-17, 3-44, 3-56, 3-84) comes from storyboard tables and is unverified. ep02 landmarks.md cites a script file outside the allowed reads, which was not opened.
