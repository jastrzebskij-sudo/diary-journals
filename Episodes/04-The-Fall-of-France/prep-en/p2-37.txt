May seventeenth, nineteen forty, Friday I was at the doctor's after my leave; he didn't examine me, just took my word for it. In the evening we arrange a feast at our quarters consisting of scrambled eggs made from twenty eggs and a tomato salad.

May eighteenth, nineteen forty, Saturday Corporal Dzierzyk still hasn't returned and the command has sent out a wanted notice. I am on duty as the company's duty NCO.

May nineteenth, nineteen forty, Sunday The company received light machine guns and is to maintain anti-landing readiness for the whole week.

May twentieth, nineteen forty, Monday Ever worse news from the front. The Germans took the cities of Amiens and Arras. Swiatkowski and I cook scrambled eggs from forty eggs. I sent Swiatkowski to the field for chives and he brought me onions, claiming that this was chives, only old. I fried the eggs and they were very tasty. In the evening, after the news bulletin in which there was news of the taking of Arras, I am very despondent and keep thinking about Irenka Ciemniakowna, because the poor girl is surely in the area now occupied by the Germans.
