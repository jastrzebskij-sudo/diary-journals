One of them started combing his hair and couldn't pull the comb back out, even though the hair was no longer than half a centimeter , it was that thick and curly. They are very disciplined, only uneducated. In the evening we invited a few of them into our barrack. At first they were very shy, but once we treated them to wine, and made a little chain bracelet for one of them, they loosened up and talked freely, and we had no small amount of laughter at their expense. They come from Sudan, and have two or three wives each, or even more, depending on how rich a man is. They are of various faiths; several are Catholic, and these wear medallions and little crosses. We laughed quite a lot when one of our friends showed them binoculars and they looked through them. When asked how old he was, one of them answered with fits of laughter, and finally said that maybe his father knew, but he didn't, because he was small then and didn't remember. While out for a walk on the beach before evening, I saw a group of black soldiers sitting on the sand. When suddenly a French NCO came up with a camera and, without saying anything to them, wanted to photograph them, but he didn't succeed, because as soon as they saw him they scattered as if a bomb had fallen among them. After long negotiations he managed to persuade one of them to be photographed. Rumors are going around the camp that the English bombed Berlin with five hundred aircraft. That they bombed military targets in Poland and in France, and even bombed Paris. That the English, with the help of the Abyssinians, have nearly driven the Italians out of Abyssinia. And that Russia has seized Bessarabia and Bukovina from Romania, and Subcarpathian Rus' from Hungary.
