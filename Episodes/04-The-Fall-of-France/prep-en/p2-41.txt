After a long search we find it and choose ourselves a suitable spot in some park right in front of a very beautiful palace. In general the area here is very beautiful. At night I go on patrol.

June second, nineteen forty, Sunday For the first time in my life I fire a cannon. They are very accurate. In the afternoon I went out into the town and met Corporal Helik, and went with him to a café. In the café were Kurcz, Sergeant Suma, Corporal Lenczyk, Jodlowski, Komar and a few others sitting in the company of French airmen. Drinking began. I lost about two hundred francs then. Kurcz started a brawl with some civilian. And Helik apparently shouted "Heil Hitler. I was no longer present for the brawl because I was very drunk and had left earlier.

June third, nineteen forty, Monday At the morning report the corporal gave a sermon to the drunkards, because yesterday's brawl had spread widely; apparently there is to be an investigation. In the afternoon the corporal ordered all the professional soldiers to pay for their board two weeks in advance, and I was left penniless. The corporal laughed that now there would be nothing left to drink with. At noon, a German air raid on Versailles and the airfield next to us.

June fourth to June eighth While waiting for equipment we go to exercises. In the evenings, with the engineer Kaczkowski, we go every day for tomato salad.
