Besides, my godmother sent me a package containing a sweater, a wool scarf, socks, gloves, chocolate, and cigarettes.

December nineteenth, nineteen thirty-nine, Tuesday

I received a package from one of the conscripts, from the Polish Women's Circle, from Mrs. Marja Wojciechowska.

December twentieth, twenty-one, twenty-two, nineteen thirty-nine. Nothing important to note. We go to rehearsals at the cinema. Pawlowski and Matyjasek left for Angers.

December twenty-third, nineteen thirty-nine - Christmas Eve

We perform the nativity play (yasevka) at the officers' casino in the presence of President Raczkiewicz, General Sikorski, and members of the government; we gave them a surprise.

Afterward we performed it again in the Administrative Company at the Assembly Station, and then had Christmas Eve supper standing in the recreation-room tearoom.

I have never had a Christmas Eve like this one - there were a few tins of sprats, potatoes, fish, and tea. It was a fine sight to see a colleague, dressed as the King of England, eating fish while holding it in one hand and a piece of bread in the other.

After the meal - for it could hardly be called a proper Christmas Eve supper - we went to the officer cadets' casino and performed the nativity play there. At nine o'clock I was asleep.

December twenty-fourth, nineteen thirty-nine, Sunday

We are putting on a performance at the cinema at three:thirty in the evening, and a second one at eight. Taking part are also French soldiers and artists who came from Paris Radio.
