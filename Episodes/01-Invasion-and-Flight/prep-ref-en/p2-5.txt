December twenty-fifth - I am busy with performances all afternoon; at ten in the morning a transport arrived from Romania, among whom was a passing acquaintance from Bucharest, I believe a doctor from the State Forests [administration].

December twenty-sixth - Second day of the holiday, we are working.

December twenty-seventh - Nothing important.

December twenty-eighth - ditto.

December twenty-ninth - Corporal Walczak returns from leave, and on this occasion there is a feast well-lubricated with alcohol, attended by the sergeant major, Warrant Officer Czapinski, and Sergeant Pieszczuchowicz. Sergeant Winogradzki was removed from his post over some unexplained matter.

December thirtieth - Warrant Officer Grzyb, Staff Sergeant Aleksander Saganski, Corporal Tadeusz Zegarlowski, and Corporal Miklas arrived at the camp. Saganski and Zegarlowski went with me right away to Comblessac to see Lieutenant Gulin, whom we caught just as he was setting off for Guer; along the way we talked about all our acquaintances. On the way we stocked up on wine, sprats, and sugar. Zegarlowski and Saganski slept at my place. Warrant Officer Grzyb stayed with a colleague, and Miklas in the general hall.
