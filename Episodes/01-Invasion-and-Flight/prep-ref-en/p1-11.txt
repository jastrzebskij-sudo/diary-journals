I left Paris around two in the afternoon for Rennes and from there to Guer. And there a sergeant of the Polish Army already drove us to Coëtquidan. I was temporarily quartered in barrack B fifteen, from where I was assigned to the billeting platoon. After a two-week stay I was appointed by Lieutenant Colonel Hofbauer as commandant of transit barrack B fifteen. There I took Bombardier Jozef Walczak, a very honest man, as my assistant.

From the Castle unit I am the first, with number twelve sixty-two. I borrowed a passport from a gentleman I did not know at all, named Mister Sumyk, and exchanged cash. After some time the transit quarters were moved to building C one, where I am commandant of half the building. The next to arrive from the Castle unit were Wladyslaw Golebiowski and Dominik Szpak. After their discharge they had worked at the P.Z.L. plant in Mielec. I took them on as assistants and they were with me for about three weeks, but they were not suited to such duties and I sent them off to the fourth Infantry Regiment. Next to arrive were Senior Sergeant Leopold Kurnik from the Castle column, Senior Wachmistrz Jozef Zarychta with the gendarmerie, and Corporal Pawlowski and Corporal Matyjasik, both from the gendarmerie.
