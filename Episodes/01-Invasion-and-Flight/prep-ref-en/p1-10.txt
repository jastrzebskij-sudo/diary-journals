In the crush that filled the train car, my wallet was stolen, containing one hundred francs and one hundred zloty.

At dawn the next day I was in Trieste. The rail line runs above the sea along a high cliff. It was the first time I had seen the sea and I was enchanted by the sight of it. By midday we were in Venice. Then through Milan and Turin to Modane, the first railway station in France. In Modane I met Mister Krosnowski, who had left Bucharest a day before me.

In the afternoon hours we left Modane for Paris. We arrived in Paris in the morning hours. We took the metro to the other station, where we left our suitcases, and set off to see Paris. I visited the Church of the Invalides; Napoleon's tomb, like other things, was covered with sandbags for protection against bombs. I stood beneath the Eiffel Tower , one was not allowed up the tower because of the war. I saw Napoleon's Arc de Triomphe, beneath which lies the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier. The Champs-Élysées are lovely.
