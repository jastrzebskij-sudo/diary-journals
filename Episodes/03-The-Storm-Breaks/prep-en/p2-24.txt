Sunday, March third, nineteen forty

I write back to Grzeskowiak and take a little walk toward Tulette.

Monday, March fourth, nineteen forty

I received a letter from Lieutenant Gulin. Irenka hasn't written back for quite a while now, so I intend to write to her.

Tuesday, March fifth, nineteen forty , We get new officers

Wednesday, March sixth, nineteen forty - Nothing important

Thursday, March seventh, nineteen forty , Captain Kruczynski conducts a records inspection.

Friday, March eighth, nineteen forty - Nothing important.

Saturday, March ninth, nineteen forty - The first Battalion went to fetch tanks.

Sunday, March tenth, nineteen forty , I'm on duty.

Monday, March eleventh, nineteen forty

Tuesday, March twelfth, nineteen forty , Nothing important, the equipment hasn't arrived.

Wednesday, March thirteenth, nineteen forty

Thursday, March fourteenth, nineteen forty , General Maczek arrived at the camp together with some French general. Today the tanks were brought to Cenle.

Friday, March fifteenth, nineteen forty , Nothing to note

Saturday, March sixteenth, nineteen forty , We're issued armored equipment and I become a driver. After lunch I go to bathe in Orange.

twelve Sunday, March seventeenth, nineteen forty , Palm Sunday
