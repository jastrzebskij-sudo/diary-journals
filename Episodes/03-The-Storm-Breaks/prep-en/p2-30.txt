Saturday, April twentieth, nineteen forty I'm transferred to billets outside town, popularly called the "folwark" [manor farm]. We live in one small room , a kitchen, really , the four of us, that is, me, Dierzyk, Tracz, and Helik.

Sunday, April twenty-first, nineteen forty Beautiful weather; in the afternoon we ride bicycles to Gigadas for lunch. There were four of us , me, Swiatkowski, Janowski, and his friend Zbyszek. Lunch cost me fifty-six francs for the four of us. Janowski also talked me into some Kirsch, which came to another twenty francs. On the way back Janowski took a fancy to go to Orange for some k-... and off he went. Swiatkowski and I returned to Ste Cecile.

Monday, April twenty-second, nineteen forty Some major arrived, accompanied by Captain Berg (who recognized me at once) and another captain, and they were selecting drivers for the motorized cavalry.

Tuesday, April twenty-third, nineteen forty I'm given tank no. sixty-eight thousand eighty-six. Driving exams are held, on flat ground and over obstacles.

Wednesday, April twenty-fourth, nineteen forty More exams, but this time in the presence of French officers.

fifteen May first, nineteen forty I try to arrange for leave.
