Sunday, March thirty-first, nineteen forty Sr. S/Sergeant Kurnik hosts me and Wolski, along with a sergeant, a friend of his. I got thoroughly drunk.

Monday, April first, nineteen forty Sergeant Wolski was assigned to the first Company of the second Battalion.

Tuesday, April second, nineteen forty I receive back pay and current pay, totaling thirteen thirty-seven francs.

Wednesday, April sixth, nineteen forty The Germans have occupied Denmark and attacked Norway.

Thursday, April eleventh, nineteen forty First live-fire range in the mountains near Lagarde Paol.

Sunday, April fourteenth, nineteen forty Yesterday, while bathing in Orange, I bought myself a gold chain and cross, and Corporal Nocon a silver one. On Sunday we throw ourselves a little party with wine and cakes, and in the afternoon the opening of the community room , in an old wine press. A very dull program, songs and recitations. In the evening a radio bulletin from Toulouse reports the sinking of seven German destroyers in the Narvik fjord.

Monday, April fifteenth, nineteen forty Inspection of the Battalion by the French.
