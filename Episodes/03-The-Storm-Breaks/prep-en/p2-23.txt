In the evening, night exercises. The men carry them out quite willingly, regarding them more as entertainment.

Tuesday, February twenty-seventh, nineteen forty

I take up my first duty as company duty NCO in France, after a five-month break.

Wednesday, February twenty-eighth, nineteen forty

The duty private overslept and woke me half an hour late, that is, at seven. Even so, the company made it to assembly on time.

Thursday, February twenty-ninth, nineteen forty

Nothing to note.

Friday, March first, nineteen forty

During drills I lost my watch, but it was found by one of the privates. We draw our pay, totaling four hundred fifty-two francs; I set aside twenty of it for a shared supper. Everyone billeted together plays a card game called "a thousand."

Saturday, March second, nineteen forty

I bought myself a pocketknife, paying ten francs.
