May ninth, nineteen forty, Thursday I spend the whole day in Ira's company and it seems to me that I am in love. In the evening I go for another walk.

May tenth, nineteen forty, Friday The Germans attacked Holland, Belgium and Luxembourg. Before dawn an air-raid alarm woke us. After some time German airplanes flew over, which were greeted by English anti-aircraft artillery and fighter planes. Over the Lemay colony one English fighter fought a battle against three German bombers. One German bomber was shot down. Air-raid alarms continued all day, but I am more occupied with love.

May eleventh, nineteen forty, Saturday Alarms all day, as on the previous day, and the whole day similar to yesterday's.

May twelfth, nineteen forty, Sunday After the service, which I attended with Ira, I went with Mister Ciemniak around the cafés, and so we visited about five Polish cafés. The Englishmen left for Belgium yesterday. In the evening we go for a walk with Ira and her friend. We spent this evening very pleasantly and properly.
